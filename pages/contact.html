We welcome improvements to RepoLaunch source codes, use RepoLaunch to create new Software Engineering datasets, and design training (Rejection-based SFT, Reinforcement Learning, etc.) and agentic (agent memory, skills, etc.) algorithms to improve the repo build and management success rate.
